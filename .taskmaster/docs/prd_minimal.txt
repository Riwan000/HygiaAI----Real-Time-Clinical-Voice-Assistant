# HygiaAI: Qdrant-Powered Clinical Memory System

## Problem Statement
In low-resource clinics, doctors lack centralized access to prior patient cases or institutional memory. HygiaAI solves this by remembering every clinical encounter, embedding it semantically, and enabling retrieval of the most relevant prior cases in seconds.
